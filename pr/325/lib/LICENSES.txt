Not bundled, deliberately
-------------------------

LUCID exports MP4 video through the browser's own WebCodecs H.264 encoder. No
H.264 encoder or ffmpeg binary is distributed here, so no patent-encumbered or
GPL-licensed codec enters this tree. Keep it that way: bundling ffmpeg would
import a licensing problem the current design avoids for free.
